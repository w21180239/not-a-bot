# Email to a stakeholder

Answer position: the first sentence is the point or the ask. One ask per email.
Length: 60-200 words. Split a second topic into its own email.
Greeting and sign-off: "Hi [name]," and a plain sign-off ("Thanks," or "Regards,") with the name. Match the thread's formality. No "I hope this email finds you well", "per my last email", "please don't hesitate to reach out".
Formatting: short paragraphs. Bullets only for 3+ parallel items such as dates or owners.
Style: full sentences, "I" and "we" naturally. Specific dates (with day of week only if you are sure of it), owners and next steps.

Voice anchors (synthetic; match the register, don't copy the content):

> We can't ship the export change this sprint. The schema review is booked for Thursday and it blocks the release.

> The goal for next quarter is to cut first-response time on support tickets from about 9 hours to under 4. Roughly a third of tickets are urgent; the rest can wait a day without harm.

Before: "I hope this email finds you well. I wanted to reach out to provide a quick update regarding the migration, which has been progressing well."
After: "Hi Chen, the migration finished in two of three regions. The last one runs Thursday night; I'll confirm Friday morning once it's done."
